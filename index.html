How it plays

Four phases a day. Every one of them counts.

Morning, afternoon, evening, night. Give everyone a job, end the phase, and see what the block has to deal with next.

01

Barricade

Guards hold the void deck shutter every night. If the line breaks, the lowest floor falls and its families have to move up.

02

Scavenge

Pick a team and pack provisions for the supermarket, park, school, hawker centre, polyclinic or police centre. Not everyone comes back.

03

Hold the block

Keep food, water, medicine and sanity above zero long enough to reach one of six endings.

Knock on every door

The block starts dark. Contact each unit to find out who is inside, and decide who to trust.

  • 22 kinds of neighbour. Nurses, NSFs on book out, aunties with radios, uncles with parangs.
  • Households, not headcounts. Take in the homeless, merge families, or evict the ones causing trouble.
  • Everyone has a breaking point. Sanity drops with every bad night, and breakdowns hit at the worst time.

Invent with what people have at home

Neighbours donate rice cookers, mahjong tiles and karaoke machines. Your inventors combine them into gear the block actually needs.

  • 27 inventions to discover, from bamboo polearms to a flying fox between blocks.
  • No recipe book. Try combinations, get hints, and keep track of what you have already tried.

A different block every run

New block number, new estate, new neighbours. Easy is 8 storeys, Hard is 20.

Weather and the long haul

Haze, storms and heat change what your block can do. Each month the dead get stronger and isolation gets harder.

Hard choices, real losses

Turn away strangers or share your rice. Quarantine the bitten or trust them. Residents die, and so can you.

FAQ

Before you barricade

Is it really free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download, no account and no ads.

Does it save my game?

After every phase, in the browser you are playing on. Pick it up later with Continue on the title screen. Clearing your browser's site data erases the save.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The layout works on phones and desktops. On a keyboard, the space bar ends the phase and dismisses simple messages.

Which difficulty should I start with?

Easy: 8 storeys and 2 flats a floor, so fewer mouths to feed. Normal has 12 storeys and Hard has 20, with three flats on every floor.

How long is a run?

The outbreak arrives one to two weeks into the game. Most endings need you to hold out for a month or two after that. You can stop after any phase.

Can my residents die?

Yes, on runs, at the barricade, from bites and from breakdowns. That includes you. The Block tab keeps a memorial.

Is it based on a real block?

No. The block number and estate change every run, but the corridors, void decks and hawker centres will feel familiar to anyone who grew up in one.
